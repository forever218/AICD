# Design Context — AICD

## What this is

A VitePress site publishing **AICD 1.0 — 人工智能内容主动披露倡议 (AI Content Disclosure Initiative)**,
a voluntary, open standard for disclosing AI participation in content creation.

## Audience

- Content creators, journalists, researchers, bloggers, publishers who may *adopt* the initiative.
- Developers who will *implement* it (HTML meta, JSON, JSON-LD, HTTP header).
- Skeptical readers who want to know how a piece of content was made.

Reading happens in long, quiet sessions — this is a document people read top to bottom.

## Use cases

1. Read the full 13-section initiative without friction.
2. Look up a single participation level (A0–A5).
3. Generate the disclosure line / machine-readable snippet to paste into their own site.

## Brand personality

Calm, credible, principled, editorial. A printed declaration, not a product landing page.
It must feel like something you could cite — closer to a W3C spec or a literary journal than a SaaS site.

## Aesthetic direction

**"Printed declaration, delivered on the web."**

- All-serif reading experience (Newsreader for Latin + Noto Serif SC for CJK) — deliberately
  avoiding the sans-serif/Inter default of generic AI output.
- Warm off-white paper, deep ink text, hairline rules instead of cards.
- A single accent: 印章 vermilion, used as a *mark* (small square, rail fill, hairline) — never as a wash.
- Mono (IBM Plex Mono) reserved for spec-like labels, level codes and machine-readable data.
- Body text large (≈18–21px fluid) with generous leading (1.9) and a ~34em measure.
- One signature: a half-filled seal square = 人工 / AI in one block.

## Hard constraints from the user

- Font must be **larger** than default, with **more line-height**.
- Some smooth animation — transitions, entrances. No bounce/elastic.
- Clean and spare. Minimal explanatory prose. No cards-everywhere, no subtitle spam.
- Leave **room for future pagination** (folder structure + nav config must extend trivially).
- Must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Anti-goals

- Inter/Roboto, purple-blue gradients, neon glow, glassmorphism, hero-metric template.
- Emoji as iconography.
